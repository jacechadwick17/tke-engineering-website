---
title: Electrical Design Engineer
location: Houston, TX
department: Electrical
active: true
description: >
  Perform discipline engineering design for natural gas and oil facilities of
  the assigned work area and complete within planned schedule and budget, in
  accordance with professional standards, project-specific procedures using
  AutoCAD. Create one-line diagrams and interconnecting diagrams for medium to
  low voltage switchgear. Identify issues and problems, develop appropriate
  solutions, and ask for assistance when required. Coordinate design and
  engineering activities with other engineering disciplines. Monitor and report
  engineering discipline status to Project Manager. Design Electrical, Grounding
  and Hazardous plans and details in accordance with industry standards using
  AutoCAD. Ensure adherence to internal and national standards for specifying
  and maintaining equipment. Design overall electrical subsystem package for a
  project that include tasks as conduit routing, voltage drop calculations,
  cable tray layout, defining light pole locations, equipment layout, building
  penetrations for conduits, etc. Define scope of work and prepare technical
  specifications for projects in accordance with NEC. Design lighting layout for
  the site in accordance with industry requirements using the Cooper Photometric
  toolbox. Compile RFQs, bid tabulation, bill of materials and coordinate
  between vendors and clients for equipment procurement.
requirements:
  - Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering and two years' experience in a
    support role in field engineering and electrical engineering projects for
    natural gas and oil facilities using AutoCAD and professional codes and
    standards to design electrical package including one-line diagrams for
    medium to low voltage switchgear, interconnection wiring diagrams, hazardous
    area classification analysis, grounding system design, lighting layout, and
    voltage drop calculations for cable and conduit sizing.
