location: 20329 SH 249 Houston TX 77070
department: Mechanical
active: true
description: >-
  Responsibilities

  Prepare and revise piping drawings, including plans, elevations, sections, details, and isometric drawings for compressor stations, meter stations, and related facilities.

  Assist with developing piping layouts and design solutions in coordination with engineers and senior designers.

  Interpret P&IDs, equipment layouts, vendor information, and engineering markups to produce accurate drawings.

  Develop detailed drawings with proper dimensions, annotations, call-outs, and design information required for complete project packages.

  Coordinate with engineering and other design disciplines to ensure drawings meet project requirements and company standards.
requirements:
  - 1–3 years of drafting experience
  - Proficient in AutoCAD; experience with CadWorx is a plus.
  - Experience developing piping layouts and design drawings for compressor
    stations, meter stations, or similar oil and gas projects is a plus.
  - Strong attention to detail.
  - Ability to successfully complete a CAD skills assessment.
  - Ability to comprehend and interpret drawing packages, design deliverables,
    and technical documentation
